Our AI

HireStream AI leverages advanced Natural Language Processing (NLP) and machine learning techniques to analyze resumes and job descriptions. The system ensures unbiased, accurate, and fast recruitment decisions by combining multiple scoring metrics into a unified intelligent ranking model.
